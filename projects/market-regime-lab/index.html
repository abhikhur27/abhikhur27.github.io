Market Regime Monte Carlo Lab

Run hundreds of simulated investment paths that switch between rising and falling markets. Change how much is invested to see how that affects growth and losses. The results come from your assumptions, not live market data.

Set up a simulation

Bull and bear are the model's two market states. Drift is the average daily return you assume; volatility controls how widely daily returns vary.

At 100% exposure, a market move passes through at its full size. At 50%, its effect is halved. Above 100%, gains and losses are amplified.
